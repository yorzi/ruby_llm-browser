# Contributor Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussions focused on the work. Harassment,
discrimination, doxxing and disclosure of other people's data or credentials are not
acceptable. Maintainers may edit, lock or remove contributions and comments that violate
this code, and may ban contributors for repeated or severe violations.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer listed in the gemspec. Reports are handled
confidentially.
